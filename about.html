Dashlam Earthmoving (Pty) Ltd supports the people and equipment that keep mining and earthmoving operations moving. We offer an integrated range of maintenance, repair, field support and compliance services for heavy equipment and mobile fleets.

We believe good service starts with understanding the job: its demands, its site conditions and the importance of reliable equipment. Our team aims to provide practical support, clear communication and professional workmanship from first contact to job completion.
